Please read before using the app (Version 1).

1. About this app

Kuziba Mobile Money Tracker ("the App") helps mobile money agents record and report their Airtel Money, MTN Money and Zamtel Kwacha transactions. "We" means Kuziba, the developer of the App. "You" means the person using the App.

2. Permission to access your SMS messages

To fill in your transactions automatically, the App asks for permission to read and receive SMS messages on this phone. The App looks only for messages that contain "ZMW" and reads them to find the amount, commission, balance, customer name, customer phone number and transaction ID. All other messages are ignored. Messages that look like money messages but are not understood may be kept in a list inside the App so they can be fixed.

3. Where your information is kept

Your transactions and messages are stored only on this phone. The App does not send them to us or to anyone else, has no accounts, shows no adverts and shares no data. Reports (PDF or CSV) you create are saved on your phone and only you decide who receives them. If you copy messages to send them to the developer, that is your choice.

4. Your consent and control

By tapping "I Agree" you consent to the App reading and receiving your SMS messages for the purpose above. You can withdraw consent at any time by turning off the SMS permission in your phone's Settings (Apps, Kuziba MoMo, Permissions) or by uninstalling the App, which deletes its data. You can delete individual transactions inside the App.

5. Your responsibilities

Use the App only on a phone you own or are allowed to use. If it is used on a staff member's phone, you must tell them and get their consent. The App shows names and phone numbers of your customers: keep your phone locked, handle this information lawfully and fairly, and do not share it without a good reason. Follow the Data Protection Act, 2021 of Zambia and any rules set by your mobile money provider.

6. Accuracy

The App reads messages automatically and may miss or misread a message, for example if a provider changes its wording. Opening and closing balances come from the balances written in the messages. Always check important figures against your official provider statements. The App is a record-keeping aid and is not accounting, tax, legal or financial advice.

7. No connection to mobile money providers

The App is independent. It is not made, approved or supported by Airtel, MTN or Zamtel.
